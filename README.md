# 🚀 Noon Saudi Arabia Sales Intelligence Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end Business Intelligence project built using Power BI to analyze retail sales performance for Noon Saudi Arabia.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights, helping stakeholders monitor revenue, profitability, order fulfillment, customer behavior, and category performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

Retail companies generate massive amounts of transactional data every day.

Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to:

❌ Identify revenue leaks

❌ Understand profitability trends

❌ Track operational performance

❌ Detect customer behavior patterns

❌ Support strategic decision-making

This dashboard solves these challenges by providing interactive and data-driven insights.

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 💎 Revenue Leaders

The highest-performing categories were:

🥇 Fashion — SAR 47M

🥈 Electronics — SAR 45M

🥉 Beauty & Health — SAR 43M

These categories represent the strongest revenue drivers for the business.

---

### 📉 Financial Challenges Identified

The dashboard highlighted several business concerns:

⚠ Revenue decline after 2023

⚠ Order cancellation rate of 7.31%

⚠ Product return rate of 5.03%

⚠ Lower revenue contribution from Sports & Outdoors

---

### 📦 Order Fulfillment Analysis

Order Status Distribution:

✅ Delivered: 59.82%

🚚 Shipped: 14.87%

⏳ Processing: 8.28%

❌ Cancelled: 7.31%

↩ Returned: 5.03%

The analysis indicates strong fulfillment performance while identifying opportunities for reducing cancellations and returns.
